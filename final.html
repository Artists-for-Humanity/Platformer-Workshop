Vanilla JS Platformer Demo

Move with A/D or Arrow Left/Right. Jump with W, Arrow Up, or Space. This is intentionally simple so it can work as a workshop starter kit.

How this is structured:
1. World data lives in arrays.
2. Player movement uses velocity + gravity.
3. Platform collision only really cares about landing from above.
4. Camera follows the player horizontally.
5. You can swap the colored rectangles for Kenney sprites later.
